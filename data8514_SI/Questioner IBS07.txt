- Special notes on structural changes vs. 2006 Economic Census and vs. 2005 annual survey:
  - Returns to "Survei Tahunan" branding (annual survey, not census)
  - Survey year shown in full: "2007" (not just last 2 digits "05")
  - Variable codes ABSENT — unlike 2005 and SE06, this form uses plain question labels with no machine-readable variable codes
  - Q10: Company Status on cover page (PMDN/PMA/Non-fasilitas from BKPM-D) — same as 2007 cover redesign
  - Q11: Location of Company on cover page (same as 2005+)
  - Workers table (Q4): simplified back to production/other × male/female matrix; exclusion notes retained; no educational breakdown (education breakdown was SE06 only)
  - Wages (Q5): simplified to 2-column (wage/salary + other incentives) × 2 worker types; the 5-row detailed breakdown (overtime, bonus, pension, accident) was SE06 only
  - Fuel (Q6): 8 items (Coke, FO/MFO grouped into "other fuels"; Charcoal/Firewood dropped); same as 2005 essentially
  - Other expenses (Q11): 6 categories; simpler than SE06 but same 6 as 2005 annual survey
  - Raw materials (Q12): 11 rows; exclusions noted (packaging, fuel, tools); same structure as 2005
  - Production (Q13): 10 rows; per-product export percentage column added (vs. binary export flag in 2006); same as 2005 reduced-row format
  - Capacity utilization (Q14): retained
  - Other income (Q15-Q16): 2 items (manufacturing services, other income combined); electricity sold now in Q10 (moved earlier)
  - Inventory (Q17): 3 stock types, same structure
  - Fixed capital (Q18): simplified — columns are purchase/addition, sale, and total estimated value (loses the detailed purchase-new/used/repair-by-others/repair-own breakdown of SE06)
  - Investment section: ABSENT (was absent in 2005, present in SE06 only)
  - Balance sheet / financial structure: ABSENT (was SE06 only)
  - Business constraints, R&D, IT use: ABSENT (was SE06 only)
  - Certification date: /2008 (certifying 2007 data in 2008)
