Q4. Apakah perusahaan melakukan kegiatan Penelitian dan Pengembangan (Litbang)?
    Is your company doing research activities?
    [ ] 1 Ya/Yes  [ ] 2 Tidak/No  [RESECH]
    [Note: RESECH RESTORED in 2011 — was removed in 2010, present in 2009]

    Definition: Creative work undertaken on a systematic basis to increase stock of knowledge and use this knowledge to devise new applications.
    Types of R&D:
    - Penelitian Dasar / Basic research (theoretical/experimental, no intended application)
    - Penelitian Terapan / Applied research (directed to specific practical purpose)
    - Pengembangan Eksperimental / Experimental development (systematic use of existing knowledge to generate new materials/products/equipment, establish new processes/systems, or substantially improve existing ones)

Q5. Apakah perusahaan melakukan kegiatan inovasi?
    Is your company doing innovation activities?
    [ ] 1 Ya/Yes  [ ] 2 Tidak/No  [INNOVA]
    [Note: INNOVA is NEW in 2011 — not present in any prior year]

    Definition: Innovation is implementing something new or a significant development in the product (goods and services), process, marketing methods, or method of organization or external relation.
    Types of Innovation:
    - Inovasi non-teknologi / Non-technological innovation:
      o Inovasi organisasi / Organizational innovation
      o Inovasi pemasaran / Marketing innovation
    - Inovasi teknologi / Technological innovation:
      o Inovasi produk / Product innovation
      o Inovasi proses / Process innovation
